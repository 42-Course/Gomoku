Expand description

Negamax with alpha-beta. Returns (score, best_move_at_this_node).

incoming_mv is the move that led to this node, used only by the observer. It’s None at the root.

§Panics

Panics if Game::undo_move fails after a successful Game::play_move; that combination should be impossible and indicates a bug elsewhere.
